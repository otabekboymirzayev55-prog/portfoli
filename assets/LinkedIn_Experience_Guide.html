📋 Add the AI/ML Course to Your LinkedIn

A short guide for students. Make it yours — don't copy each other word-for-word. Recruiters notice identical profiles.

1. The basic entry

On LinkedIn → Add profile section → Experience. Fill it in like this:

Title AI/ML Trainee
Company IT Park Uzbekistan
Employment type Apprenticeship  (or Trainee — not "Internship")
Location Tashkent, Uzbekistan
Dates Apr 2026 – Present
⚠️ Don't write "Internship" unless you actually did real work for a company. "Apprenticeship" or "Trainee" is honest and still looks professional. A title you can't defend in an interview hurts you.

2. The description template

Use this shape, but fill in your own words and your own projects:

AI/ML track of the "Uzbekistan Digital Inclusion" program (IT Park × World Bank) — hands-on, project-based.
  • [pick a verb] real ML projects end-to-end and published them on GitHub
  • Worked with [list 3–4 tools YOU actually used]
  • [pick a verb] a [your favorite project]
  • Studied [the topics you've reached]
🔗 GitHub: [your link]

3. Word bank — so nobody sounds identical 🎲

5. Three rules ✅

1Use YOUR real projects + YOUR GitHub link. That's what makes you unique automatically.

2Change the verbs and order. Don't all write "Learning fundamentals of ML."

3Stay honest. List only what you actually did — it must survive an interview question.

💡 Pro tip: attach Media to the entry — link your GitHub and a project. A project link is worth more than any sentence.
