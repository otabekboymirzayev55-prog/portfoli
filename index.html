A rare blend of hands-on industrial experience and formal business education — bringing both operational rigor and strategic thinking to every challenge.

My career began not in a classroom but on the production floor — operating high-precision machinery, managing teams of technicians, and ensuring that every finished product met uncompromising quality standards. Those two years at Maftunkor Print taught me something no textbook can: that operational excellence is built through daily discipline, clear communication, and zero tolerance for process drift.

Today I'm formalizing that practical foundation through a Bachelor's in Business, Administration, and Law at Tashkent State University of Oriental Studies. My goal is to combine operational depth with strategic and legal literacy, making me effective across the full lifecycle of a business — from process design to governance.
